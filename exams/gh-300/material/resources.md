## Usage notes

- The official study guide reflects skills measured as of August 7, 2026.
- Microsoft Learn defines the exam objectives; GitHub Docs defines current
  product behavior.
- Internal guides, slides, and recordings are supplemental. When they conflict
  with first-party documentation, follow the first-party source and flag the
  discrepancy.
- Do not treat these resources as a local question bank. Author original,
  cited practice questions until a legitimate GH-300 bank is added.
